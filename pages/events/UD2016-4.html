Right Speech and Living with Ourselves – From the Mind to the Mouth, What Happens?
Ajahn Pasanno and Ajahn Jotipālo
Upāsikā Day, Oct. 8, 2016
Abhayagiri Buddhist Monastery in Redwood Valley, California and online
4 sessions, 39 excerpts, 2:19:03 total duration

Ajahn Pasanno and Ajahn Jotipālo lead teachings and discussion on how speech that contributes to harmony with others and happiness with ourselves begins with attention to and cultivation of internal mental processes.
