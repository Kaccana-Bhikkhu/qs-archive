A Monastic's Life
Ajahn Pasanno and Ajahn Yatiko
Upāsikā Day, Jun. 15, 2013
Abhayagiri Buddhist Monastery in Redwood Valley, California
7 excerpts, 17:24 total duration
Show featured excerpt (1)

Ajahn Pasanno and Ajahn Yatiko describe how the monk’s discipline has influenced Thai culture and reflect upon how Westerners could integrate elements from the monastic form into their lives. The available recording contains only the first session.
