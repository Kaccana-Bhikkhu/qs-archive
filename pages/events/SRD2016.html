The Anxiety of Impermanence and the Impermanence of Anxiety
Ajahn Pasanno
Spirit Rock Daylong, Jul. 2, 2016
Spirit Rock Meditation Center in Woodacre, California and online
2 sessions, 17 excerpts, 1:11:30 total duration

Ajahn Pasanno leads a day of teaching and meditation exploring impermanence and how it can be contemplated in a way that leads to the end of suffering rather than causing anxiety.

If I meditate and get out of thoughts, I have less suffering, but I don’t really have much happiness; I simply have less suffering.
