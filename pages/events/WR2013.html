The Fourth Foundation of Mindfulness – Abhayagiri Winter Retreat 2013
Ajahn Pasanno, Ajahn Karuṇadhammo and Ajahn Jotipālo
Winter Retreat, Jan. 1, 2013 to Mar. 31, 2013
Abhayagiri Buddhist Monastery in Redwood Valley, California
44 sessions, 166 excerpts, 14:23:02 total duration
Show featured excerpts (5)

Mindfulness of dhammas as described in the Satipaṭṭhāna Sutta (MN 10) encompases the Five Hindrances, Five Aggregates, Six Sense Bases, Seven Factors of Awakening and the Four Noble Truths. Ajahn Pasanno, Ajahn Karuṇadhammo, and Ajahn Jotipālo take turns reading suttas and teachings about these subjects.

Original recordings available on www.abhayagiri.org
