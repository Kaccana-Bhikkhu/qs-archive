13. Comment: I always enjoy coming [to Abhayagiri] and the questions and answers in Berkeley because it’s such a happy place. I volunteer at a homeless ministry in the Tenderloin in San Francisco, and it’s not a happy place. The leaders are ex-Franciscan nuns. Their faith is very strong in the face of suffering, and that’s what inspires me to participate in an environment that’s frightening, dangerous, and unwholesome. The inspiration of being around others who have faith helps me to go beyond my own life experience, and this seems like a very important part of the ability to give.
