The Anxiety of Impermanence and the Impermanence of Anxiety, Session 4 – Jul. 2, 2016

Download audio (9:26)
1. “With great respect and listening carefully, I’m having a hard time. I have looked at impermanence for a long time, but once I got older and turned 70, I see clearly that things I would have grasped at and thought would make me happy will not. They all change or they pass and go away. I know that I want to control, and I see that that’s not there. I just fractured my back, so my body is not a place where I have control. If I meditate and get out of thoughts, I have less suffering, but I don’t really have much happiness; I simply have less suffering. I see and am close to many people who have meditated a lot longer than I have. I don’t mean to be judgemental, but I don’t see them much happier than I am. It’s discouraging. …” Answered by Ajahn Pasanno.
