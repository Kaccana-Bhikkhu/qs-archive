16. “Was the fire sermon (SN 35.28 (Chanting Book translation) a ‘teaching moment’ of hyperbole? Does the Buddha’s recipe for liberation truly entail cutting off this experience of beauty? Where’s the Theravāda equivalent to the Zen ‘suchness’ —the awe of life and its ephemerality? Thank you!” Answered by Ajahn Pasanno.
