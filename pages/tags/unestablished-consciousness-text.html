8. “During walking meditation, I was immersing my attention into the body and sounds, particularly trying to experience consciousness. At one of these moments that sound hit my awareness, I zeroed in on experiencing the pure consciousness mechanism before sound hit it. It was very subtle. Immediately I felt an unpleasant feeling in my stomach that resembled fear. I got disconnected from it and was wondering why fear would show up, as if I was close to seeing something that the little me wasn’t ready for. … Is it possible to experience consciousness without its object? …” Answered by Ajahn Karuṇadhammo.
